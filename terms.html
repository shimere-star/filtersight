FilterSight

Terms of Service

FilterSight provides one subscription with DNS filtering and support features to help adults manage access to adult content.

Plans and billing

FilterSight costs $10 per month. By completing checkout, you authorize Stripe to charge the displayed subscription price each month until cancellation.

Filtering limits

FilterSight works at the DNS/domain level. It can block known adult domains, but cannot distinguish adult content from other content when both are served from the same general-purpose website or app. Filtering depends on device and network configuration. You are responsible for installing and keeping the profile enabled. A profile can be removed, and FilterSight is not a substitute for professional or emergency support.

AI companion

The companion is a limited-purpose conversational feature for support in the moment. It is not medical care, therapy, or emergency service. Contact local emergency services or a crisis resource if you or someone else is in immediate danger.

Private journal

The private journal is optional. It lets you keep a personal goal, a coping plan, and journal entries in your member account. The journal is a self-reflection tool, not medical care, therapy, or a guarantee of any outcome.

Do not enter website addresses, domains, browsing history, or DNS queries in journal fields. FilterSight screens for this information, but automated screening is not perfect.

Your journal is not sent to the AI companion automatically. You control whether to separately submit any information to the companion. While your subscription is active and you can sign in, you can export your journal as JSON, delete individual entries, or delete all journal and coping-plan content. Deleting journal content does not cancel your subscription or delete separate account, billing, or DNS-profile records.

Cancellation

You can request cancellation from your member dashboard after signing in by email. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and FilterSight does not charge a cancellation fee. Subscription charges already made for the current period are not automatically refunded. Journal and coping-plan content is scheduled for deletion from the active application database no later than 30 days after the subscription ends. If you cannot sign in and want earlier journal deletion, contact support from your subscription email address.
