Plans and billing

Plan features and recurring prices are shown at checkout. By completing checkout, you authorize Stripe to charge the displayed subscription price on the recurring schedule until cancellation.

Filtering limits

Filtersight works at the DNS/domain level. It can block known adult domains, but cannot reliably distinguish adult material from other material hosted on the same domain. Filtering depends on device and network configuration. You are responsible for installing and keeping the profile enabled. A profile can be removed, and Filtersight is not a substitute for professional or emergency support.

Optional SMS and partner alerts

SMS is optional and is not a condition of purchase. If you opt in to your own messages, message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. For partner alerts, the partner must reply YES to a separate invitation before any alerts are sent; the partner may reply STOP to opt out.

AI companion

The companion is a limited-purpose conversational feature for support in the moment. It is not medical care, therapy, or emergency service. Contact local emergency services or a crisis resource if you or someone else is in immediate danger.
