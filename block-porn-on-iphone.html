How to block porn on your iPhone with FilterSight

FilterSight uses a DNS configuration profile to block adult websites on your own phone, with a separate member space for private check-ins and AI support.

You do not need an accountability partner or a separate filtering app. You do need a FilterSight membership and permission to install a profile on your device.

1. Get your FilterSight profile

Open FilterSight in Safari on your iPhone and complete signup. After your payment is verified, the setup page prepares your personal DNS profile. Select Download Profile to download filtersight.mobileconfig.

If your payment succeeds but profile preparation fails, contact support@filtersight.com for help rather than buying another membership.

2. Install it in Settings

Downloading a profile does not install it. Open your iPhone’s Settings, select Profile Downloaded when shown, and review the profile before following the installation prompts. You can view profiles under General → VPN & Device Management.

Apple explains the installation process in its configuration profile guide. If the downloaded profile expires before installation, download it again.

3. Check your setup

Confirm that the FilterSight profile appears in Settings. Open NextDNS’s connection test to check whether that browser connection is using NextDNS. This checks the DNS connection; it does not prove that every adult domain or every app is blocked.

If filtering seems inactive, note whether you are using Wi-Fi or cellular data and whether a VPN or another DNS service is active, then contact support for help checking the configuration. A test on one connection does not verify all your connections.

What does DNS porn filtering do?

DNS helps your device find a website’s address. FilterSight configures encrypted DNS requests to NextDNS, where the adult-content category is blocked. This works at the domain level: it is not a tool for inspecting every image, video, or post.

Know the limits before you subscribe.

The FilterSight profile is removable. Filtering depends on the connection using the configured DNS service, and an allowed site can still contain adult content. FilterSight is not an undeletable blocker and does not promise complete coverage across all browsers and apps.

Private support alongside filtering

Your member dashboard provides structured check-ins and an AI companion when you want to pause or talk through a moment. DNS query logging is disabled, and no accountability partner receives browsing reports. Read the privacy policy for how account, journal, and companion information is handled.

Is FilterSight the right fit?

FilterSight is designed for voluntary porn blocking on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with private support in a member dashboard. The membership is $10 per month. If your requirement is a filter you cannot remove, this setup does not meet that requirement.
