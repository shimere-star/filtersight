Privacy Policy

FilterSight processes information needed to provide your subscription, DNS filtering, and support features. This policy is informational and is not a claim of legal compliance.

Account and billing

We use your email address and subscription status to provide your account, email sign-in links, and support. Sign-in links expire and can be used once. Stripe processes checkout and recurring payments; FilterSight does not receive your full payment-card number.

DNS filtering and logs

To block domains, NextDNS must process your DNS queries when they are made. FilterSight disables DNS query logging and does not use browsing activity to build advertising profiles.

Check-ins, exercises, and cooldowns

When you use the check-in feature, your selection exists only for your current Streamlit browser session so the feature can work. It is cleared when you sign out or the application session resets, and it is never written to FilterSight's database. We never retain structured check-in selections.

We don't keep records of which exercises you start, finish, or stop. Exercise activity exists only while you're doing it and is never retained. Cooldowns are temporary session-based timers. Cooldown status exists only for your current session, is never written to FilterSight's database, and does not resume if the session resets.

Private journal and coping plan

If you choose to use the private journal, FilterSight stores the goal, “why it matters,” coping actions, and private notes you enter. Those free-text fields are encrypted at rest before they are written to the journal database. FilterSight does not accept website addresses, domains, browsing history, DNS queries, location, or social-media activity in journal fields.

Some journal information remains readable in the database so the feature can operate: your subscription email address, entry IDs, dates and times, selected category and action labels, approximate encrypted-field lengths, rate-limit records, and retention dates. Encryption protects the content fields, but it does not hide this operational information.

Journal content is not sent to Anthropic automatically. If you separately choose to discuss journal information with the AI companion, the AI companion rules below apply to the text you submit.

While signed in, you can export your journal and coping plan as a JSON file, delete individual journal entries, or permanently delete all stored journal and coping-plan content. These controls do not delete account, billing, DNS-profile, or service-security records.

AI companion

Your current message and relevant recent conversation history are sent to Anthropic to generate a reply. FilterSight does not save companion conversations in its database. Anthropic may process or retain API inputs under its applicable terms and configuration. Don't include information you don't want processed for that purpose.

Retention and security

We keep your account information while your subscription is active and as needed for legal, dispute, and security purposes. Check-in selections, exercise activity, and cooldown status are never retained beyond your session. Companion conversations are not saved in our database.

Journal and coping-plan content is kept while your subscription is active. When the subscription ends, FilterSight schedules that content for deletion from the active application database no later than 30 days afterward. If you cannot sign in after cancellation, email support@filtersight.com from your subscription email address to request earlier journal deletion. Support must verify the request before using the protected deletion process.

Deleted journal content may remain for a limited time in hosting-provider backups or volume snapshots according to the provider's backup schedule. Those backups are outside the journal feature's direct control and are not used to restore an individual deleted journal.

No sale of personal information

We don't sell your personal information — including check-ins, exercise activity, or AI conversations — to anyone, for any purpose. We share limited data only with the service providers needed to run FilterSight (payments, DNS filtering, AI replies, email delivery, and hosting), and only for operating the service.
