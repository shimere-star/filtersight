Privacy Policy

Filtersight processes information needed to provide subscriptions, DNS filtering, and the features included in the plan you choose.

Account and billing

We use your email address and subscription status to provide your account, email sign-in links, and support. Sign-in links expire and can be used once. Stripe processes checkout and recurring payments; Filtersight does not receive your full payment-card number.

DNS filtering and logs

Each customer receives an individual NextDNS profile. Filtersight disables DNS query logging for both plans and does not use browsing activity to build advertising profiles.

AI companion

If you use the Tier 2 companion, your messages and relevant conversation history are sent to Anthropic to generate replies. Do not include information you do not want processed for that purpose.

Retention and security

We retain account information while needed to provide the subscription, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. We use service providers such as Stripe, NextDNS, Anthropic, email delivery, and Railway to operate the service. We apply access controls and reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.
