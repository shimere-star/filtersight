Why Filtersight Exists

The problem

Compulsive porn use doesn't stay contained. It eats time. It erodes confidence — especially in real relationships, with real people. It's not about willpower failing once; it's about a pattern that's hard to see your way out of when you're in it alone.

What we built

A filter that actually holds. Filtersight blocks adult content system-wide on your iPhone — not just in Safari, across apps. It's not about shame. It's about removing the moment of easy access, so the decision to stop is easier to make than the decision to keep going.

An accountability partner — if you want one. You can choose someone you trust to get notified if you try to bypass the filter. This is optional. Not everyone's ready to loop someone else in yet, and that's okay — the filter still works without it. When you are ready, having a real person in the loop changes the stakes in a good way.

A text, not a lecture. If you try to get around the filter, you get a message — picked at random from a hundred different ones, so it never feels like a canned response. Not preachy. Just a reminder that you're still in this, and one moment doesn't undo the work you've already put in.

Someone to talk to, even at 2am. Not everyone has a person to call in the moment they're struggling. Filtersight's AI companion is there for exactly that — a steady, non-judgmental presence when you need to talk it through and there's no one else awake.

The $5 to cancel isn't a fee. It's a pause button. Canceling takes a small step — either your accountability partner gets a heads-up, or there's a small charge to process it. That's intentional. It's not about the money. It's about making sure canceling is a decision you actually mean, not something you do in a moment you'll regret ten minutes later.

Who this is for

People who are tired of the pattern and want something real between them and the next click — with or without someone else watching.
